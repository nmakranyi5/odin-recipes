Super Easy Egg Casserole

Description

This egg casserole is a perfect breakfast or brunch dish. It's loaded with eggs, cheese, and your favorite vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 6 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 1 cup chopped vegetables (bell peppers, onions, spinach)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together the eggs and milk. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Stir in the shredded cheese and chopped vegetables.
  4. Pour the mixture into a greased baking dish.
  5. Bake for 30-35 minutes, or until the eggs are set and the top is golden brown.
  6. Let it cool for a few minutes before slicing and serving. Enjoy!
